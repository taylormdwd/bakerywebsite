Our Sweet Services

✿

Every creation is made from scratch with premium ingredients and a whole lot of love.

Custom Cakes

From elegant tiered wedding cakes to fun birthday creations, every cake is designed around your vision. Choose your flavors, fillings, and frosting — we handle the rest.

Cupcakes

Beautifully decorated cupcakes made to match any theme or color scheme. Perfect for parties, events, or just because.

Custom Cookies

Decorated sugar cookies by the dozen or half dozen, individually packaged to match your event theme. Pricing varies based on design complexity.

Wedding & Bridal Cakes

Your wedding cake should be as unforgettable as your day. We create elegant, custom bridal cakes that taste as incredible as they look.

Party Bundles

Our most popular option! Get everything you need in one package — custom cake, cupcakes, cookies, and matching décor. Bundles start at $150–$175.

Custom Décor & Goods

Complete your party look with custom decorations designed to match your baked goods — including cake toppers, banners, cupcake toppers, and more.
